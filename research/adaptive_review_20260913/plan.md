Three fixed methods will be evaluated on identical eligible origins:
1. conservative: zero-return point, original frequency probabilities and bounds.
2. adaptive_mix: combine incumbent, frequency, previous candidate and zero-return point forecasts using only losses whose target_end is strictly earlier than the current origin minus one hour. Use a 365-day window and 90-day half-life for horizons below 14d; 1,095-day window and 365-day half-life for 14/30d. Normalize point/range losses by the corresponding past frequency loss. Softmax sensitivity is fixed at 8. Point priors: zero .5, incumbent .25, frequency .125, candidate .125. Probability priors: incumbent .5, frequency .4, candidate .1. Range priors: equal thirds. No weights are chosen using a future test year.
3. adaptive_risk: retain adaptive_mix's point forecast; combine its probabilities 50:50 with a monthly logistic recalibrator fitted to earlier fully matured, actually generated adaptive_mix forecasts (C=.1). Re-estimate bounds from those past point errors divided by origin-time trailing 720h volatility times sqrt(h), using weighted 10th/90th residual quantiles and the same fixed windows/half-lives. Enforce bounds around the original point. This is an empirical delayed residual wrapper, not a claim to reproduce ACI or inherit a distribution-free coverage guarantee.

Require at least 90 matured prior daily origins below 14d and 365 at 14/30d before scored comparisons. Both new adaptive methods generate causal warm-up outputs, but warm-up scores are excluded for every comparator. Every prediction records maximum feedback target_end, feedback count and calibration cutoff. New alert thresholds use past negatives of each method, require 40 negatives, and target a 5% empirical false-positive tail; report recall as well, so fewer alerts cannot be sold as better discrimination.

Primary criteria are separate price MAE, path-event Brier and proper 80% interval score. Always include incumbent, previous candidate, frequency and no-change baselines, with identical dates and truth.
