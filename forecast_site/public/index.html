Methodology and limits
  • Walk-forward validation retrains on the past and predicts the next unseen 30-day block.
  • Purged gaps remove overlapping target windows; embargo rows reduce autocorrelation leakage.
  • Feature selection happens inside each training fold, not on the full dataset.
  • Full-run result on 2026-05-02 passed both horizons, but the edge is modest. 30-day direction remains fragile.
  • This is statistical research, not financial advice.
Forecasts can be wrong, especially during ETF-flow shocks, FOMC repricing, liquidation cascades, regulatory news, or exchange outages. The dashboard shows confidence and validation because the correct default is skepticism.
