Your privacy

Privacy policy

This notice explains the information involved when you visit the public Ethereum forecast dashboard at etherforecast.live.

Draft prepared 12 September 2026 · Effective date to be set when published.

Draft for operator review. Confirm the operator's name and a private privacy-contact email before publication. This file is outside the public website.

1. The public dashboard

You can read the forecasts without creating an account. The current dashboard has no registration, newsletter subscription, customer profile, payment form, or personal-information submission form.

Forecast probabilities, price ranges, and historical results describe the ETH market. The forecasting pipeline uses market data, not a visitor's identity or browsing activity, to generate these public forecasts.

2. Information involved in a visit

Loading a page sends a network request to our hosting provider. That request includes an IP address and may include browser and device information, the requested URL, request time, and referrer information. Technical information can be processed to deliver the site, maintain availability, investigate errors, and prevent abuse.

The dashboard's own page code does not set cookies, use browser local storage for visitor profiles, or embed advertising pixels, Google Analytics, or session-replay scripts. Your browser may cache public files. Hosting infrastructure and external services can handle technical request information under their own policies.

3. Hosting and external services

The dashboard connects to the following services. A direct browser connection discloses your network address and normal request metadata to the receiving service.

Coinbase and CoinGecko

Coinbase privacy policy · CoinGecko privacy policy

These providers operate international infrastructure. Technical information may be processed outside your country. Their policies explain their locations, safeguards, and handling of information; a provider's own account or trading features are separate from using this dashboard.

4. Storage and retention

The public forecast files and market research records are maintained separately from website request information. This dashboard does not create a customer database for its visitors.

Infrastructure providers manage their technical records according to their service settings and policies. Retention varies by record, provider, and applicable obligations; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion period for records controlled by those providers.

5. Optional donations and other links

If donation addresses are displayed, they are public receiving addresses. The dashboard does not ask you to connect a wallet, enter a private key, or provide card details. A donation sent through your own wallet produces a public blockchain transaction, which can include sending and receiving addresses and transaction amounts. Blockchain records cannot be erased by this website.

Other websites linked from this dashboard apply their own privacy practices when you visit them.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can control cookies and cached data in your browser and block external requests with browser privacy tools. Blocking fonts, chart libraries, or price APIs can affect their corresponding features.

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or to object to processing. Contact the operator about information under the site's control, or the relevant provider about information it controls.
